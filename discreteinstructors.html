Active Discrete Mathematics

For Instructors

Everything you need to actively engage students. Free. Just click to start.

Adopt Active Discrete Mathematics as your main text. Or, use the activities to accompany your text.

A fresh take on discrete content.
Content that fits

Includes the skills and concepts that computer science and mathematics want. Focused on the core ideas so you can cover the entire text in one semester. Clearly labeled Detours and Excursions allow you to safely skip topics as desired.

Good stuff first

Counting, graph theory, and number theory come early, capturing student interest right away. That gives you rich, interesting topics to draw on when you get to the foundational material: logic and proof, sets and relations, functions, and recursions.

Algebra review built in

Explains the algebra skills students need as they go, in discrete contexts. That makes the course accessible to students without calculus (The ACM recommends precalculus as a prerequisite), and helps students whose algebra skills are a bit rusty.

Pedagogy that engages students.
Ready-to-use activities

Multiple, well-tested in-class activities per section, plus about one extended puzzle per chapter. Easy for new instructors to incorproate active learning. Ideal at whiteboards in smaller classes or at seats with a neighbor in larger classes.

Carefully scaffolded

Activities designed to promote student thinking with parts that grow from easy to medium to sideways to challenging. Exercises are clearly labeled: Practice, Understand, Recap, Explore so students know what to expect.

Inquiry-driven

Emphasizes conjecture, counterexample, and reasoning -- not just procedure. Students deeply engage in rich mathematical sense making by doing mathematics in collaboration with classmates.

Built for every student.
Free to use

All students will have the textbook -- no access codes, no accounts, no exceptions. No student falls behind beacause they couldn't afford the book.

Accessible materials

Built in PreTeXt for easy use with screen readers and Braille. Collapsible examples, solutions, proofs, and hints limit online scrolling. Quick jump-to references support just-in-time review of course concepts.

Inclusive approach

Explicitly invites students in to try the math. Algebra review sections and scaffolded activities and exercises help every student experience success. Uses multicultural names and relevant contexts throughout.
