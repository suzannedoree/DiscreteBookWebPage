Active Discrete Mathematics

For Instructors

Students learn mathematics by doing mathematics.

The big ideas of discrete mathematics, curated to fit in a single semester. Built accessible to students with varied mathematical backgrounds. Rich with engaging narrative, detailed examples, scaffolded in-class activities, and interesting exercises. That's Active Discrete Mathematics. It's fun. And it works.

How is this discrete math textbook different from all other discrete math textbooks?

Content that fits

Includes what computer science and mathematics courses need -- and you can cover the entire text in one semester.

Good stuff first

Counting, graph theory, and number theory come early, capturing student interest right away -- and gives you rich, interesting topics to draw on when you get to the foundational material: logic and proof, sets and relations, functions, and recursions.

Algebra review built in

Explains the algebra skills students need as they go. That makes the course accessible to students without calculus (ACM reccommends precalculus as a prerequisite), and helps students whose algebra skills are a bit rusty.
