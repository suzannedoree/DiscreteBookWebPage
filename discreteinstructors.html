Active Discrete Mathematics

For Instructors

Students learn mathematics by doing mathematics.

The big ideas of discrete mathematics, curated to fit in a single semester. Built accessible to students with varied mathematical backgrounds. Rich with engaging narrative, detailed examples, scaffolded in-class activities, and interesting exercises. That's Active Discrete Mathematics. It's fun. And it works.

Fresh take on content

Content that fits

Includes the skills and concepts that computer science and mathematics want. Focused on the core ideas so you can cover the entire text in one semester. Clearly labeled Detours and Excursions allow you to safely skip topics as desired.

Good stuff first

Counting, graph theory, and number theory come early, capturing student interest right away. That gives you rich, interesting topics to draw on when you get to the foundational material: logic and proof, sets and relations, functions, and recursions.

Algebra review built in

Explains the algebra skills students need as they go, in discrete contexts. That makes the course accessible to students without calculus (The ACM reccommends precalculus as a prerequisite), and helps students whose algebra skills are a bit rusty.
