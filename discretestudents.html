Geting the most out of this text.

Go to class

You paid for class. Class is your chance to learn from an expert (your prof or TA). So, be there. If you occasionally miss, read the textbook, ask a classmate for notes, and visit office hours for specific questions.

Do the activities

Each section of the textbook contains activities. This is your chance to do the math. Partner with a classmate and get started. Go at a pace where you both understand. Don't be afraid to test out ideas. And ask for help when you need it.

Read the textbook

Read the textbook. Start with the Definitions -- do you know what each word and notation means? Next, try the Examples -- use the Solutions to help. Last, don't forget the important stuff: Theorems summarize big ideas.

Do your homework.

The secret to learning? Practice, practice, practice. The exercises are your chance to practice, deepen your understanding, and explore new ideas. Avoid the temptation to use AI. Instead use the Hints, work with a classmate, or go to office hours.
