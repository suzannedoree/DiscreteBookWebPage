Geting the most out of this text.

Go to class

You paid for class. That means you get to hear from an expert (your prof or TA). Be there. If you occasionally miss a class, read through the textbook, ask a classmate for notes, and ask any lingering questions during office hours.

Do the activities

Each section of the textbook contains activities. This is your chance to do the math. Partner with a classmate and get started. Go at a pace where you both understand. Ask for help when you need it. But try it on your own first.

Read the textbook

Read the textbook. Start with the Definitions -- do you know what each word and notation means? Next, try the Examples -- use the Solutions to help. Last, don't forget the important stuff: Theorems summarize big ideas.

Practice, Understand, Recap, and Explore

>The secret to learning discrete mathematics? Lots of practice. Do the exercises to practice, test your understanding, and explore. Avoid the temptation to get help from AI. When you get stuck, use the Hints, ask a classmate, or go to office hours.
